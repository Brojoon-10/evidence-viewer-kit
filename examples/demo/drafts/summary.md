# Bike Lane Pilot — Summary Draft

## 1. What we found

○ Traffic and safety findings
 - Average weekday cyclist count rose from 310 to 455 after the pilot lane opened {@g1-1}
 - Near-miss reports dropped by about one third {@g1-2}
  · Most remaining reports were at the Elm Street intersection {@g1-3}

○ Resident feedback
 - 68 % of surveyed residents supported keeping the lane {@g2-1}
 - Main concern: loss of 14 parking spaces {@g2-2}

## 2. Recommendation

○ Make the lane permanent, built to the design guide
 - Widen to 1.8 m with a 0.6 m buffer (Guide 3.2) {@g3-1}
 - Add a protected corner at Elm Street (Guide 4.1) {@g3-2}
